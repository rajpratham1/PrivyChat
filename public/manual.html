📡 How to Connect Locally (No Internet)

  • WiFi Mesh: Both devices must be on the same WiFi router or one device acts as a mobile hotspot. Open the app, go to "Nearby" tab — peers appear on the radar automatically.
  • Bluetooth BLE: Enable Bluetooth on both phones. Tap "BLE Scan" in the Nearby tab to discover and pair with devices within 10–30 meters.
  • Air-Gap QR: No WiFi or Bluetooth needed. One device shows a QR code; the other scans it with the camera. A secure encrypted channel is established with zero radio transmission.

🔐 Mobile Security Spec

  • Encryption: ECDH P-256 key exchange + HKDF SHA-256 derivation + AES-256-GCM (with AAD).
  • Perfect Forward Secrecy: Every session uses a fresh ephemeral keypair — previous sessions cannot be decrypted even if current keys are compromised.
  • Zero Storage: Messages are never written to the device disk. All state lives in volatile RAM.

1. Getting Started: The "Search Engine" Interface

PrivyChat disguises itself as a simple search engine. Here is how to navigate the "Google Theme" UI to access different modes.

🌍 Public Lobby (Group Chat)

Want to join the global conversation?

  • Action: Leave the search bar empty and click the button.
  • Result: You enter the "General" room. Anyone can join here.
  • Security: Encrypted in transit (HTTPS), but not end-to-end encrypted. Public by design.

🔐 Private Room (Password Protected)

For secure team meetings or group chats.

  • Action: Click or type a Room Name in the search bar and hit Enter.
  • Setup: You will be prompted to set a Password.
  • Encryption: Uses AES-GCM (military-grade). The password generates the key. If you lose the password, the data is unrecoverable.

🎲 1-on-1 Secure Link ("I'm Feeling Lucky")

The gold standard for top-secret conversations.

  • Action: Click the button.
  • Result: Instantly generates a unique, 1-time room (e.g., secure-x8a92b).
  • Sharing: Copy the URL and send it to your partner. The encryption key is hidden in the URL hash, so the server never sees it.
⚠️ IMPORTANT: PrivyChat uses RAM-Only Storage. If you refresh the page or the server restarts, your chat history is permanently deleted.

5. Troubleshooting & FAQ

Microphone / Camera Not Working?
Make sure you have allowed "Microphone & Camera Access" in your browser's site settings.
(Click the Lock/Tune icon in the address bar -> Site Settings -> Microphone/Camera -> Allow)
Encryption Error?
If you see "Decryption Failed" messages, it means your partner has a different room password. Ensure you both joined with the exact same password or verify your safety numbers in Nearby Mesh.
