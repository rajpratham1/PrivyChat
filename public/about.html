About PrivyChat

The ultra-secure, RAM-only spy messenger for when privacy is a matter of survival.

Why We Built This

RAM-Only Architecture

No hard drives. No databases. Every message exists only in volatile memory. Pull the plug, and it's gone forever.

Self-Destruct

Messages that burn after reading. Set timers from 5 seconds to 1 minute. Leave no digital footprint behind.

Stealth Mode

Disguise your chat as a Calculator or a Weather app in one click. Perfect for prying eyes.

Integrated Ecosystem: SecureChat

The Permanent Secure Messenger

We have seamlessly integrated SecureChat to provide robust, End-to-End Encrypted Text Messaging. While PrivyChat focuses on ephemeral video/voice, SecureChat ensures your text conversations are safe, built by Ayush (Arya).

  • 💾 Persistent Secure Storage
  • 📱 Group Chats & File Sharing
  • 🌐 PWA Support (Installable)
